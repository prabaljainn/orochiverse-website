Mark exploration · 9 directions

Concepts for the orochi mark.

Each option riffs on the same brand DNA: convergence, the number eight, serpent/strand, and a unified core. Wordmark stays constant — only the glyph changes.

01
01 · The Convergence Eight strands meet at one node.

Eight curved threads converge to a single ink dot — most literal interpretation of the eight-headed serpent unified into one body. Reads as a logo and as data viz.

02
02 · The Knot An eight folded into itself.

A figure-8 knot inside the orbit — eight as infinity, woven. Distinctive, animatable as a single continuous stroke. Works small.

03
03 · The Compass Eight directions from one core.

Eight equal radials with a single accent ray — directional, technical, evokes a compass rose and a sun. Closest cousin to the current mark, more refined.

04
04 · The Coil A serpent, coiled inward.

A continuous spiraling path with a single head-dot — the orochi distilled to gesture. Feels organic next to the rest of the system's geometry.

05
05 · The O A monogram with a cut.

A solid O ring with an accent bar slicing through — a literal mono­gram for "Orochi" with the diagonal energy of the current slash. Confident, less abstract.

06
06 · The Crown Heads above, body below.

Stacked arcs read as the eight heads, then collapse into a single accent stem — a body. Mythic without being literal. Strong silhouette at any size.

07
07 · The Constellation Eight points, one center.

An asterism: eight nodes orbiting a blue core, connected by faint blue threads. Reads as system, network, multiverse. Friendly to motion.

08
08 · The Fang A chevron and a stem.

A blue chevron biting into a single ink stem — directional, code-like (`>` / pipe), and reads as a stylized serpent fang. Most "tech-forward" of the set.

09
09 · The Eclipse Two bodies, one core.

An eclipsing crescent with a blue core peeking through — duality resolving into a single point. Calm, photographic, distinct from the rest of the set.
